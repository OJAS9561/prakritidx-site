How it works

Two areas of focus — skin and hair — each following the same simple path.

  1. Describe the concern

    Tell us what's going on in a few lines, and upload a selfie or an existing lab/test report if you have one.

  2. Answer a short set of questions

    A focused set of multiple-choice questions, plus an optional note for anything specific — allergies, thyroid, PCOS, medications, and so on.

  3. Get a free preliminary read

    An accurate, partial conclusion — enough to understand the direction, before deciding whether to go deeper.

  4. Unlock the full report

    A detailed report with your constitution analysis, likely causes, and specific diet and practice recommendations — generated for you after payment.
